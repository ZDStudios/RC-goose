TT;DR: Let the operator control a goose from another computer through a relay they host on Coolify, without port forwarding and without weakening existing safety rules.

## Why
The operator wants remote control of a goose on a second computer. ADR 0059 records the design.

## Scope
Opt-in `[remote]` config, a Windows runtime client that keeps one outbound WSS connection, a Node relay with a single-page panel and Dockerfile, and backward-compatible local IPC frames for walk-to, leaf piles, remote pokes and status. No change to existing CLI/IPC semantics, terminal protection or manners. macOS/Linux clients wait for their own tray indicators.

## Plan
Relay and panel first, then engine and IPC, then config, client and tray, then end-to-end checks and Coolify deployment notes.

## Acceptance
Remote control is off by default, visible in the tray while active, disconnectable from the tray, refused during quiet manners except calm-down, and every existing check still passes.
